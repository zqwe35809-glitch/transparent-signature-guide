# How to Create a Clean Transparent Signature for Word & PDF Documents

When handling contracts, proposals, or agreements electronically, signing with a digital facsimile of a handwritten signature is often required. However, photographing a paper signature with a smartphone usually introduces gray paper backgrounds, shadows, or yellow tints that cover document text.

## Best Practice Workflow

1. **High-Contrast Source**: Sign with a black gel or felt-tip pen on clean, unlined white paper. Avoid textured or colored paper.
2. **Proper Lighting**: Take the photo under diffused daylight to minimize gradient paper shadows.
3. **Dedicated Background Thresholding**: Standard AI background removers designed for portraits or products often mistake delicate pen stroke endpoints for noise, leading to jagged or missing strokes. Using a specialized signature transparency tool like [SignatureClear](https://signatureclear.online) separates pen strokes from paper and paper noise, converting white paper and gradient lighting into an alpha transparency channel while preserving stroke nuances.
4. **Document Insertion**: Insert the resulting transparent PNG into Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or your PDF editor. Set the text wrapping to "In Front of Text" so the signature overlays naturally on top of signature lines without obscuring any background text.
